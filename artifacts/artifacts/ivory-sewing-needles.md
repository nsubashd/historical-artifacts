
# Ivory Sewing Needles

**Period:** Ancient / Historical  
**Material:** Ivory  
**Object Type:** Sewing Tool

## Description

These finely made needles were used for sewing and textile work before the widespread use of modern metal needles. Their small size and carefully shaped form demonstrate the skill involved in producing practical tools from ivory.

## Historical Context

Sewing needles are among the most important tools in the history of clothing and textile production. Objects such as these provide evidence of how people produced, repaired, and maintained clothing and other textiles.

## Historical Significance

Although small and easily overlooked, sewing needles offer a glimpse into everyday life in the past. They represent the craftsmanship, technology, and domestic activities of the people who made and used them.
