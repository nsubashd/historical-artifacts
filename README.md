# Historical Artifacts

A visual collection of historical artifacts, objects, inscriptions, tools, and everyday items from different periods of human history.

## About This Project

This project explores the stories behind objects from the past — what they were made from, how they were used, when they were created, and what they can tell us about the people who used them.

## What You'll Find

- Ancient and historical artifacts
- Tools and everyday objects
- Historical inscriptions
- Weapons and military objects
- Clothing and personal items
- Museum discoveries
- Unusual objects with interesting histories

## Purpose

The goal is to make historical objects easier to understand through short descriptions, visual documentation, and historical research.
